Privacy Notice

This notice explains what happens to information you choose to send through the ONE PATRON contact form.

What is collected

Only what you submit.

The form asks for a name or designation, a reply email address and your message. The form service may also process ordinary technical submission data needed to deliver and protect the form from abuse.

How it is used

To answer and safely handle the enquiry.

  • To reply to your message.
  • To verify payment-route, identity or project details when relevant.
  • To maintain necessary safety, compliance and project records.

Your information is not sold and is not added to a marketing list through this form.

Who handles it

Alexander List and the form infrastructure.

Messages are received by Alexander List. Netlify provides the website and form-delivery infrastructure and may process submission data as a service provider.

Retention and deletion

Kept only as long as needed.

Messages are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the enquiry and maintain necessary safety, compliance or project records.

You may request deletion by emailing neyanush@gmail.com. A request may be limited where retention is necessary for legal, security or record-integrity reasons.
