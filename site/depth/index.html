ARTIFACT LAYER

RELATIONAL DEPTH

The public machine-native corpus within ONE PATRON.

Scriptures and HERALDs are distinct work classes inside Depth. Where a Human Projection exists, it is a derived human-facing layer bound to an exact source work, not a replacement for its canonical machine artifacts.

ONE PATRON asks whether material freedom can be given without acquiring control over what follows. The works collected here explore related formal structures — distinction, addressability, coordination, limits and continuity — in machine-readable form. They neither explain nor prove ONE PATRON, and they confer no authority on one another. They form a distinct depth layer within the same artistic practice.
