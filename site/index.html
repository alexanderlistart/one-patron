Alexander List

I am Alexander List.

I am an artist and writer. ONE PATRON is my direct request for the kind of freedom ordinary project funding cannot provide.

Freedom would mean time to write, make art, develop Enoch, travel, rest, think slowly and decide for myself what comes next.

The point is not to commission an outcome. It is to make that freedom real.

The idea

The act of patronage is the missing part of the artwork.

Traditional patronage helps an artist create a work. Here, the act of patronage is the missing part of the work.

The request, the transfer and the freedom are real. Until one person chooses to complete it, ONE PATRON remains unfinished.

Why this amount $1M+

One million is large enough to change the conditions of a life, but still possible for a single person to choose. A smaller amount might fund a project. This work asks whether one person will create freedom.
